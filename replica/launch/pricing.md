## Why no paid tier (standing rule)

The commissioner's rule (2026-11-24): *every part free forever, no tiers,
no payments, no license server — ever.* This overrides any monetisation
suggestion, including future ones. Consequently:

- **No Stripe.** No products, no prices, no webhooks. (replica-deploy's
  payment section is N/A by design.)
- **No "Pro" language** in listings or landing copy.
- Revenue, if ever wanted, would have to come from something that does not
  break the rule (e.g. optional donations) — out of scope, not planned.
